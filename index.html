Engineering A Healthcare Legacy.

Founded in 2014, Crescent Care (M/s CrescentCare - AS Medical Infra Pvt Ltd) delivers comprehensive turnkey hospital superstructures, modular operating theatres, and clinical facilities across India.

Led by Mr. Prithvi Singh (Civil & Heavy Hospital Superstructures) and Mr. Sandeep Tiwari (Modular OTs, MGPS & Clinical MEP Systems), our multidisciplinary engineering firm executes seismic-compliant medical buildings built strictly to NABH, HTM-02, and NFPA standards.
