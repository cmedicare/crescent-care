About Crescent Care

Crescent Care (registered as AS Medical Infra Pvt Ltd) is a healthcare and hospital construction company established in 2014 in New Delhi. We specialize in civil construction, modular operation theatres, and clinical MEP systems for medical facilities across North India.

Our core work includes turnkey hospital construction, Medical Gas Pipeline Systems (MGPS), cleanroom HVAC with HEPA filtration, and specialized hospital interiors compliant with NABH and HTM standards.

We have executed projects for major institutions including AIIMS Awantipora, Doon Government Medical College, Akhand Jyoti Eye Hospital, and various private multi-speciality hospitals.
