About Crescent Care

Crescent Care is a premier civil construction and infrastructure development company established in 2014 in New Delhi. We specialize in comprehensive civil engineering for infrastructure projects across North India.

Our core capabilities are Civil & Structural, MEP Engineering, Interior Work, Finishing Work, Elevation Work, Landscaping Work, IT Work, and Facade Work.

We have executed projects for major institutions including AIIMS Awantipora, Doon Government College Campus, Akhand Jyoti Institutional Complex, and various large-scale commercial and residential infrastructure projects.
